How the server's weights move: FedAvg, FedProx, FedAvgM, FedAdam, FedYogi

Each curve is the global model of one strategy (mean of the seeds), compressed from all weights to 2 coordinates with one PCA shared by every curve. Drag the slider, press play, or scroll over the chart to move through the rounds. Solid: real runs. Dashed, lighter: at every round, the model another strategy would produce from the base run's model in one step; the base then continues with its own update.
